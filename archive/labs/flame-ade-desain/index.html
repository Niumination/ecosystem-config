Security first

Your keys, your data, your machine. No exceptions.

OS Keychain
API keys stored exclusively in your operating system's keychain — never in localStorage or on disk.
Path Traversal Protection
Canonicalize + deny-list prevents any attempt to access files outside your project.
Zero Telemetry
No analytics, no tracking, no account required. Your development activity stays on your machine.
SSRF Defense
DNS rebinding and SSRF protection on all AI HTTP requests. Sandboxed web preview iframe.
CSP Hardened
Content Security Policy prevents XSS and injection attacks across the entire application.
Signed Releases
Every release artifact is Minisign-signed so you can verify authenticity before installing.
